    How a house gets on the list

    1. New listings, daily

      Single-family, multi-family, condos and townhouses across Rhode Island, pulled each morning.

    2. Compared to sold prices

      Price per square foot against recent sales in the same town and property type, not against other asking prices.

    3. Run as a flip and a rental

      After-repair value, repairs by age of the house, a 70%-rule max offer, then rent, cap rate and monthly cash flow.

    4. Scored and tracked

      Foreclosures, price cuts and stale listings score higher. When a flagged house sells, we log what it sold for.
